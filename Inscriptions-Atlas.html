READING THE ATLAS

Evidence, placed in context.

This working catalogue brings together local inscriptions, outside references and later records. Dates and identifications remain qualified where the sources disagree.

What does a point mean?

A point can represent a published monument location, a user-supplied locality, a nearby landmark, or a broad regional reference. The number of decimal places is not evidence of survey accuracy. A town reference does not identify the original digging point or today's museum.

Keep the unmapped records

Records without a defensible location remain in the list. Select “Without coordinates” to find them. Uncertain date ranges overlap the chosen years; undated records stay visible unless the principal-period catalogue is selected.

Source collection

Each record links to its source and full summary where available. The catalogue is not certified exhaustive. Modern country and district shapes are geographical context, not historical borders.
