How this map was drawn

The Karatoyā and Dikkaravāsinī markers are broad reference anchors. Kañjagiri, Dikṣu and the southern confluence remain outside the map as direction cards because their modern locations have not been established here. No kilometre scale or area calculation is attached to the sketches.

The four-colour layer follows Neog’s sequence. Its north–south edges and connecting seams are drawn for explanation; they are not supplied by the text. The outer enclosure is a separate illustration of wider textual reach. The two are not identical.

Yojana measurements remain in their original unit. Modern country lines, district lines and rivers are context. They do not establish ancient sovereignty. The inscription layer preserves the catalogue’s location uncertainty and never assigns records to a pīṭha using the drawn shapes.
